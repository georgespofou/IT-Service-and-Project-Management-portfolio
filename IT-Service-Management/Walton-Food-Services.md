# Walton Food Services: IT Service Management Case Study

## 1. Business Scenario

Walton Food Services uses an in-house marketing and customer records system developed five years ago. The software experiences capacity issues, causing repeated outages during peak working hours. When the system is unavailable, staff maintain paper records temporarily.

## 2. Incident Management

An incident is an unplanned interruption to a service.

**Example:** The marketing and customer records system becomes unavailable during peak working hours.

**Recommended action:** Record the incident through the service desk, assess its impact and urgency, and work to restore the service.

## 3. Problem Management

A problem is what causes one or more incidents.

**Example:** The software's capacity issues may be causing repeated outages. The exact root cause needs investigation.

**Recommended action:** Investigate system capacity, review incident records and identify the underlying cause.

## 4. Workaround

A workaround is a temporary way to reduce the impact of an incident when a permanent solution is not yet available.

**Example:** Staff maintain paper records while the software is unavailable.

**Limitation:** This approach can be time-consuming and may increase the risk of errors or lost information.

## 5. Incident Prioritisation

Two factors to consider are:

- **Impact:** How many staff members and business activities are affected by the outage.
- **Urgency:** How quickly the service needs to be restored, particularly during peak working hours.

## 6. Recommendations

- Log incidents through the service desk.
- Investigate the recurring capacity issues.
- Monitor system performance during peak periods.
- Assess long-term capacity improvements.
- Review incidents and identify opportunities for continual improvement.

## 7. Learning Outcomes

This case study demonstrates my understanding of incident management, problem management, workarounds, incident prioritisation and continual improvement in IT service management.

## 8. Skills Demonstrated

- ITIL 4 concepts
- Incident and problem analysis
- Risk awareness
- Business impact assessment
- Technical problem investigation
- Documentation and communication
